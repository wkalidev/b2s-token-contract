# B2S Tokenomics

## Supply
- Total Supply: 400,000,000 B2S
- Decimals: 6
- Initial mint: 400M to contract owner

## Distribution
- Daily rewards: 5 B2S/user/day
- Bridge rewards: 10 B2S per verified bridge
- Staking rewards: 12.5% APY base

## Burn Mechanism
No burn mechanism currently.
Governance can propose burn via DAO.

## Fee Structure
- Marketplace: 2.5% platform fee
- Liquidity pool: 0.25% swap fee
- Bridge: 0.3% fee (reduced for high volume)
