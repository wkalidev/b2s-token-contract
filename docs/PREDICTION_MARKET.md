# Prediction Market

## Contract
SP936YWJPST8GB8FFRCN7CC6P2YR5K6NNBAARQ96.b2s-prediction-market

## How it works
1. Admin creates prediction question
2. Users bet YES or NO with B2S tokens
3. After resolution, winners split the pool
4. Platform fee: 2.5%
