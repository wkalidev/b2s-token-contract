# Prediction Market

## Contract
SP1V72500C63KN9E348QDK9X879MASSTN0J3KBQ5N.b2s-prediction-market

## How it works
1. Admin creates prediction question
2. Users bet YES or NO with B2S tokens
3. After resolution, winners split the pool
4. Platform fee: 2.5%
