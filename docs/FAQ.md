# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is $B2S?
B2S is the native token of the Base2Stacks ecosystem.

## How do I earn B2S?
- Claim 5 B2S daily via claim-daily-reward
- Stake tokens to earn 12.5% APY
- Track bridge transactions to earn rewards

## What wallets are supported?
Leather and Xverse wallets on Stacks mainnet.

## Is the contract audited?
All contracts use safe math via stacks-clarity-toolkit.
No recursive functions. Admin functions are protected.

## What is the total supply?
